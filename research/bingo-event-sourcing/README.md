# Conversation bingo: state and replay boundaries

**Status:** Exploratory research, not a specification

**Researched:** 2026-11-25

**Repository snapshot:** Katakit `486c868` (`jude/event-sourcing`); pre-migration comparison `458b53c`; Katabase `2b045e4b`. At final verification, Katakit `origin/main` had advanced to `d8b2ff1`; its Bingo, core, ingestion and persistence source trees were identical to the researched checkout.

## Research question

Which Bingo facts cannot be reconstructed from the existing transcript log, and what would be lost by replacing the current subjects? The [spec](../../specs/bingo-event-sourcing.spec.md) makes the design decisions; the observations below explain their basis.

### Why follow the Deck rather than copy a whole-state plugin?

Kataboard's [event design rules](https://github.com/katabase-ai/protozoa-app/blob/2b045e4b00d5f41f7ef35a0dbf672aa8fb5c7b9b/pkgs/fe/src/docs/dev/architecture/application-systems/kataboard/event-sourcing/event-design.md) choose one event per independently valid change. The [Deck method](https://github.com/katabase-ai/protozoa-app/blob/2b045e4b00d5f41f7ef35a0dbf672aa8fb5c7b9b/pkgs/be/src/kataboard/lib/br/system/Deck/methods/methods.ts) emits `slideRemoved`; its [structure projection](https://github.com/katabase-ai/protozoa-app/blob/2b045e4b00d5f41f7ef35a0dbf672aa8fb5c7b9b/pkgs/be/src/kataboard/lib/br/system/Deck/observables/%24deckStructure.ts) also removes an emptied column. The event states the action; replay derives its required consequences. A deal clearing its old overrides is the Bingo equivalent. A forced mark is different: speech cannot tell us whether the player intended it, so it must be recorded.

Kataboard's [reconstruction rules](https://github.com/katabase-ai/protozoa-app/blob/2b045e4b00d5f41f7ef35a0dbf672aa8fb5c7b9b/pkgs/fe/src/docs/dev/architecture/application-systems/kataboard/event-sourcing/event-sourcing.md) also require coherent observations at every event and distinguish reconstruction from live work. That rules out sampling two uncoordinated “latest” values or replaying a shuffle or recognizer callback. Kataboard's Postgres storage, schema catalog and board identifiers serve different needs; Katakit already has a per-runtime history.

These are baseline checks, not evidence of a completed migration. No microphone, Chrome capture, published package, or historical user storage was exercised. A retained segment array cannot reveal past manual edits, evicted words or the precise time a silence timer originally fired. The scoped guarantee is exact replay from **recorded events**, not recreation of that missing history.
